About Our Church

Rooted in Faith, Growing in Grace

Discover who we are, our divine calling, and our open embrace for every individual and family.

✝

A Spiritual Home for Every Family in Mangadu

At Glorious Zion Church (Mangadu Ministry), we believe that everyone matters to God. Led by Pastor P. Titus, our sanctuary is a refuge of prayer, hope, and genuine fellowship for seekers and believers alike.

Established in Puduvattaram, Mangadu, Chennai, our ministry ministers to the spiritual and practical needs of our neighborhood. Whether you are seeking spiritual salvation, praying for physical healing, needing guidance for your children, or desiring a close walk with Jesus, our doors are open to welcome you.

🌟 Our Vision

To see every soul redeemed by Jesus Christ and every family established in the peace and holiness of God.

🎯 Our Mission

Uncompromising Bible preaching, steadfast fasting and prayer, vibrant worship, and caring fellowship.
